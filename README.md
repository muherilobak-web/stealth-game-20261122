A top-down 2D stealth game: Wren slips into a great candlelit archive by night to steal the pages of the Starless Atlas — and slip out again, unseen. Three hand-designed rooms, patrolling wardens with raycast vision cones, suspicion that builds and fades, lantern light and deep shadow, fern thickets to hide in, and a coin to throw when a patrol needs a reason to look the other way.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests, no libraries — just the game.

- **WASD / Arrow keys** — move
- **Shift (hold)** — sneak: slower, silent, harder to spot
- **Click a floor tile** — throw a distraction coin (3 per level, within about 7 tiles)
- **Esc** — pause
- **M** — mute
- **R** — restart the level

Collect every atlas page in the room, then reach the green exit door — it stays locked until the pages are yours. Fill a warden's suspicion meter and the alarm goes up: every warden hunts your last seen position, and a touch while the alarm is up means you are caught. Break sight for about 5 seconds and the alarm fades. Sneak and stand still inside a fern thicket and no warden can see you at all.

## Scoring

Pages are worth points, plus a time bonus, a ghost bonus for finishing a room without ever raising the alarm, and a bonus for unspent coins. Finish all three rooms for a final rank: **Ghost** (never caught in a full alarm), **Shadow** (caught once or twice), or **Seen**.

## Original work

Every name, character, level, drawing, and sound in this game is original. All audio is synthesized live with WebAudio — oscillators and filtered noise only, no audio files and nothing copied from any commercial game.
